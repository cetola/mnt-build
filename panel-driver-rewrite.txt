Just a quick note to remind myself what's going on here, as this branch is
persisting longer (sadly) than I thought it would.

The patches essentally add "knobs" to turn that edit how the panel driver's
timings work, or when they occur.

The panel-updates and lpc-updates folders hold scripts that will edit the kernel
in such a way as to take advantage of a new lpc driver. This requires an update
to firmware which can be found here:
https://source.mnt.re/stephano/pocket-reform/-/tree/sysctl-fw-panel-v2?ref_type=heads

This firmware change allows all "levers" of the display to be controlled by the
kernel, rather than having part of the control in the firmware and the rest in
the kernel.

For whatever reason, on my Pocket Reform display, applying the kernel patches
makes the display much more reliable. If I had to guess this is because the
timings are indeed a bit off. I have to then guess that the reason only some
folks see issues is because some panels are more sensitive than others.
Regardless, keeping these patches on my system helps.

Adding the lpc-updates and panel-updates on the other hand doesn't seem to help
at all. Regardless of how I change those "knobs" I cannot get the panel to
reliably come up every time. It still blanks out occationally.
